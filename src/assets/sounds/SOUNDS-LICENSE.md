# Sound effects

All files here are from two packs by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), released under
Creative Commons Zero (CC0): free to use in personal, educational and
commercial projects, no credit required.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

They were converted from Ogg to 16-bit mono WAV (44.1 kHz), with leading and
trailing silence trimmed and a 2 ms fade-out, so every browser (including
Safari) can play them.
